# Content needed from Hoffstot's Cafe Monaco

A homepage preview built from what is publicly on hoffstots.com as of 2026-09-05.
Nothing on the page is invented.

## Things on their current site they will want to know about

Their site is **not neglected** — it is added to and never cleared out. The
`NO DRIVE THRU 9/6` notice is for tomorrow, so somebody is keeping it current. The
problem is that nothing is ever removed, so today the homepage carries all of this
at once:

- **"MAKE YOUR FATHER'S DAY RESERVATIONS TODAY!"** — in September.
- **Two contradictory drive-thru notices**: `**NO DRIVE THRU 2/8**` from February, and
  `NO DRIVE THRU 9/6` for tomorrow, sitting a few lines apart.
- **"WE ARE OPEN LABOR DAY"** — Labor Day has passed.
- **Two stacked blocks of hours**, plus a loose `Sunday 1pm-8pm` with no label.
- **The word `restaurant_menu` printed on the page**, where an icon font failed to load
  and the ligature name rendered as text instead.
- **The footer reads © 2018.** Fixed here, computed at build time.

None of it is on the preview. A single "what's on this week" area that one person edits
would solve the whole category of problem.

## The big one: every menu is a PDF

All seven menus — lunch, dinner, early bird, family style, Sunday pasta drive-thru,
Prime Rib Mondays, dessert — are PDF downloads. That means:

- On a phone they open in a viewer, pinch-to-zoom, and are painful to read.
- Google cannot index a single dish, so nobody searching "prime rib Oakmont" finds them.

The preview lists the seven menus but does not reproduce their contents, because the
prices and dishes should come from them rather than be transcribed out of a PDF by us.
**Getting the menus into real web pages is the single highest-value change available.**

## Not used, because we could not verify it

- **"Open since 1971."** This is in our own research notes and is very likely true, but
  the year **appears nowhere on their own website**, so it is not on the preview and is
  not in the outreach email. If they confirm it, it belongs in the hero.
- **Who owns it.** No owner or manager is named anywhere on the site. Our notes flag a
  possible change of ownership. Nobody is named on the preview and the email opens
  "Hello," rather than guessing.

## Photography

They have effectively **no photographs on their homepage** — the only real image on the
site is one 2020 shot of the storefront, which is genuinely good and is used here as the
hero. Everything else is logo files.

For a restaurant this is the biggest missing asset after the menus. Photographs of the
dining room, the bar, and half a dozen actual dishes would change the page completely.

## Ordering

Online ordering runs on **SpotOn** and works. The preview links straight out to it and
does not try to replace it.

## Logo and colours

Their logo is a good one — a sage-green tree with a strong serif wordmark. The white
plate behind it was knocked out so it can sit on a cream header, and the baked-in phone
number and web address were cropped off the bottom of the lockup, since both already
appear in the top bar and go illegible at header size. The original file is untouched on
their server.

Colours are taken from their own storefront and logo: the black and gold of the window
trim on Allegheny Avenue, the sage green of the tree, the terracotta of the planters.
